Question 04

Working method

Interviewer

How does he handle reliability?

By making failure behaviour part of the design, not an afterthought.

  1. 01
    Integrate

    Connect language, vision, and speech providers behind explicit interfaces.

    provider layer
  2. 02
    Retrieve

    Assemble ranked, bounded, source-aware context for the task.

    context layer
  3. 03
    Validate

    Use schemas, deterministic checks, retries, and human approval.

    control layer
  4. 04
    Evaluate

    Measure quality, latency, cost, and failure behaviour before claims.

    evidence layer

At VolyxAI, an independent product effort, I apply this method across language, vision, and speech systems. Visit VolyxAI ↗
