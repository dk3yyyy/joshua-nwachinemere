At VolyxAI, an independent product effort, I apply the same four-part method across language, vision, and speech systems.

  1. 01
    Integrate

    Connect language, vision, and speech providers behind explicit interfaces.

    provider layer
  2. 02
    Retrieve

    Assemble ranked, bounded, source-aware context for the task.

    context layer
  3. 03
    Validate

    Use schemas, deterministic checks, retries, and human approval.

    control layer
  4. 04
    Evaluate

    Measure quality, latency, cost, and failure behavior before claims.

    evidence layer
