Privacy

What this site does with your data.

The short version: no analytics, no advertising cookies, no trackers, and no account. The portfolio guide is the one place where a question leaves your browser, and the raw question is not sent to the AI provider.

Last updated 9 November 2026

What the site collects

This is a static portfolio. It does not ask you to create an account, set first-party analytics or advertising cookies, or run a tracking script.

Cloudflare hosts the site and processes requests so pages can be delivered. Cloudflare may process standard technical request data, such as an IP address, browser headers and the requested URL, under its own terms. I do not use that data to build a profile or to advertise to you.

The portfolio guide

The “Ask Joshua” panel answers questions from a fixed set of reviewed portfolio records. When you submit a question, your browser sends it to this site’s server. The server validates it locally and applies sensitive-data and prompt-injection checks before any model call.

If a safe question reaches the AI routing step, the server sends Cloudflare Workers AI a set of fixed routing labels, a request-scoped subset of reviewed evidence IDs, and short unverified excerpts from public portfolio sources. Your raw question and its wording are not sent to the model. The model may only choose from that bounded evidence set; the answer and citations you see are rebuilt from the reviewed records on the server.

The site does not intentionally store your question. Cloudflare processes the request as the hosting and AI provider under its privacy policy. The guide keeps a 500-character limit, has no file upload, and should not be used for confidential or personal information.

If Workers AI is unavailable, the API returns no model-routed answer and the page falls back to deterministic retrieval in the browser. Model routing is disabled in production until an edge rate-limit binding is configured. If Workers AI is not configured at all, the server can use a Groq adapter with the same bounded inputs; the provider receives the same reviewed evidence and public source excerpts, never your raw question.

Email

If you use the email link, your email provider and mine process the message so it can be delivered and answered. I use it to reply to you, not to add you to a mailing list. There is no newsletter and no marketing automation on this site. The downloadable CV is a public PDF and includes the professional email and phone number printed on it.

Who is responsible

Joshua Nwachinemere, founder of VolyxAI, is responsible for this portfolio. You can reach me at joshua0nwachinemere@gmail.com.

I do not keep a first-party database of visitors or guide questions. Cloudflare’s retention of technical request data is governed by its privacy policy. If you email me, I keep the message only as long as needed to answer and to keep a record of the conversation.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU, you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data you have sent me, and you can object to how it is used. Email me with the request. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Links to other sites

Project, writing and profile links open other sites, including GitHub, LinkedIn, Hashnode and the Volyx Lens site. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may set their own cookies. This policy only covers this portfolio.

Your choices

You can browse the entire portfolio with JavaScript disabled. The guide will be unavailable, but the work, writing, background and contact details remain readable.

If you have a question about this policy or want to ask me not to keep an email, write to joshua0nwachinemere@gmail.com.
